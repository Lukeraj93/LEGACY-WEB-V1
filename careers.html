Coach Careers at LEGACY+

We hire coaches who care about standards, service, teamwork, and the long game. Credentials matter, but character, floor presence, attitude, and the ability to genuinely improve lives matter more.

Coach Standards

What We Look For In Every LEGACY+ Coach

A strong coach here does more than run a good session. They steady clients, elevate the room, and make the team stronger around them.

We do not care how many clients you have or had. We care how many clients' lives you are changing.

Character First

We want integrity, calm confidence, consistency, and a coach clients can trust.

  • You do the right thing when no one is watching.
  • You take ownership of your sessions, your prep, and your follow-through.
  • You can be direct without being arrogant.

Team Player By Default

We do not hire lone wolves. We hire people who make the floor better for clients and teammates.

  • You communicate well and share information clearly.
  • You support the team standard, not just your own schedule.
  • You handle pressure without creating drama on the floor.

Lives Changed Over Client Count

We care about the quality of impact, not inflated numbers or empty sales language.

  • You know how to build adherence and confidence, not just intensity.
  • You can point to meaningful client progress, not just session volume.
  • You understand that retention is earned through service and results.

Coachable And Professional

The best coaches still take feedback, keep learning, and show up prepared.

  • You welcome feedback and apply it fast.
  • You respect systems, timing, and the standard of the business.
  • You bring the right energy even on demanding days.
Requirements

What You Need To Bring

We hire for a mix of baseline qualifications, coaching craft, client care, and the way you operate inside a team-led environment.

Baseline

Qualifications & Safety

  • Recognised coaching or personal training certification.
  • Current first aid and CPR, or readiness to renew immediately.
  • Clear understanding of scope and when to refer out.
Delivery

Coaching Craft

  • Strong cueing, progressions, regressions, and session pacing.
  • Ability to coach beginners without overwhelming them.
  • Ability to keep experienced clients progressing with clarity.
Service

Client Care

  • Punctuality, preparation, and professional communication.
  • Session notes, follow-ups, and real attention to adherence.
  • Calm, respectful energy with different personalities and needs.
Fit

Attitude & Team Fit

  • No ego-first behaviour and no territorial mentality.
  • Willingness to support teammates and protect floor standards.
  • Hunger to grow, stay accountable, and represent the brand well.

Open Tracks

Current Coaching Roles

If you fit one of these lanes and you align with the standard above, we want to hear from you.

Full-Time

1-on-1 Performance Coach

Lead structured private coaching for working adults who need accountability, strength progress, and high service.

  • Comfortable coaching body composition, strength, and general performance goals.
  • Able to manage progression reviews and long-term client adherence.
Specialist

Pre & Postnatal Coach

Coach women through pregnancy and post-birth training with care, clarity, and strong session judgment.

  • Relevant population knowledge and safe coaching decision-making.
  • Strong communication style for nervous or returning-to-training clients.
Specialist

Longevity & Older Adult Coach

Build confidence, capability, and long-term strength for older clients who need coaching they can trust.

  • Good pacing, regressions, and communication with older populations.
  • Comfortable prioritising safety, independence, and progression together.
Development

Coach Development Track

For newer coaches with the right character, work ethic, and willingness to be sharpened by a strong system.

  • Solid fundamentals, humility, and high willingness to learn fast.
  • Ready to support the floor while growing into a larger client load.
