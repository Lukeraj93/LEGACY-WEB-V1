What The Service Is

Specialized Coaching For Each Stage

This program adapts training to trimester needs and postpartum recovery goals. Every block is personalized with close monitoring, low-risk progression, and practical guidance for daily movement.

Who It Is For

  • Expecting mothers looking for safe guided training
  • Postpartum clients rebuilding strength and confidence
  • Women returning to exercise after delivery
  • Clients wanting expert supervision and clear progression
Benefits

Build Strength, Stability, and Confidence

Core and Pelvic Support

Exercises selected to protect and strengthen the core and pelvic floor systems.

Movement Comfort

Targeted mobility and posture work to reduce common discomfort and stiffness.

Confident Return

Structured progression for safe return to regular training and active lifestyle.

Program Structure

From Assessment to Stage-Based Progression

  1. Comprehensive health and movement screening.
  2. Trimester or postpartum phase-specific planning.
  3. Private coaching with weekly load and movement adjustments.
  4. Regular reassessment for confidence, comfort, and strength targets.
  5. Transition plan toward long-term fitness goals.
