Why LEGACY+ Exists

LEGACY+ was created not just for clients, but for coaches as well.

Shared Growth It is a place where both groups can level up themselves. Clients come here to learn how to take care of their health, get stronger, and be guided with real structure. Coaches come here to learn how to coach people well in an environment that allows growth for both sides.

Why That Matters The idea was to build a place where coaches felt truly valued, and where clients could feel that too. If the coaches are taken care of properly, supported properly, and held to a higher standard, the clients will feel that standard in every part of the coaching experience.

  • Clients receive high-standard, high-quality coaching.
  • Coaches learn in a place built on feedback, standards, and mentorship.
  • Both groups are expected to grow, not just show up.
  • Better-supported coaches create a better coaching experience for clients.
  • The room is meant to feel welcoming, structured, and less intimidating.
  • Clients will feel that standard in every part of the coaching experience.

The Founder's Point of View

Luke built LEGACY+ to change the standard of the fitness industry in Malaysia.

The Bigger Goal The ambition was bigger than building another gym. The idea was to create an environment where both clients and coaches could thrive. A place to learn, get stronger, and become better humans while being held to a real standard.

The Standard Clients should receive high-standard, high-quality coaching. Coaches should experience high-standard coaching, learning, and mentorship. Those two things should not be separated. They are meant to strengthen each other.

The Core Belief If the coaches are taken care of, the clients will be too. That belief sits at the center of LEGACY+ and shapes the kind of culture the brand is trying to build in Malaysia.
