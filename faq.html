Frequently Asked Questions

Find the Answers That Match Your Situation

How quickly can I start?

Most clients begin within 3 to 7 days after consultation and coach matching.

Do I need prior training experience?

No. We start with movement assessment and build your progression from your current level.

Can parents train on flexible schedules?

Yes. Session planning adapts weekly around family commitments and changing routines.

Is this safe for older adults?

Yes. Programs emphasize movement quality, stability, and progressive load management.

How does pricing work?

Packages scale by session frequency and support level. Full details are on the pricing page.

Do I need to commit long-term?

No. Start with a no-commitment consultation and choose the plan that matches your goals.

What happens during the first consultation?

We review your goals, training background, schedule constraints, and what support level fits best.

Can the program adapt if my condition or schedule changes?

Yes. Programming and session flow are adjusted as life, recovery, or training capacity changes.
