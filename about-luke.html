Founder Story

From General Fitness to Precision Coaching

Luke built LEGACY+ around one principle: clients deserve personalized, high-standard coaching instead of generic training plans. His work combines practical strength science with a coaching experience that is discreet, professional, and outcome-driven.

Over the years, he has coached professionals, entrepreneurs, and athletes with diverse constraints, from demanding travel schedules to injury history and stage-specific needs.

Coaching Philosophy

  • Clarity first: every client knows the plan and progression.
  • Safety and form are non-negotiable.
  • Strength is the foundation for long-term health.
  • Consistency beats intensity for sustainable results.
