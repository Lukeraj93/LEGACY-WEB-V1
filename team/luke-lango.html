How Luke coaches

Luke works best with clients who want a coach that is clear, structured, and serious about progress, but still practical enough to keep things moving when the day is messy.

He trims out the noise, gives direct feedback, and expects follow-through, because the goal is not just a good session. It is repeatable progress that actually shows up in real life.
