Things You Didn't Know About Shobana

Likes

Evidence-based training, honest effort, and clients who want their sessions to mean something.

Dislikes

Wasted reps, lazy structure, and pretending "good enough" is the same thing as real progress.

Pet Peeve

Calling a set "easy" when the standard clearly slipped halfway through. Shobana definitely noticed.

Hobbies

Strength training, refining programmes, and staying close to the performance side of sport.

Most People Wouldn't Guess

She has competed in CrossFit and at the Powerlifting Nationals, so she understands performance pressure from both sides of the clipboard.

Quirk

There is usually just enough banter between hard sets to keep things human, then she is right back to the standard.

How Shobana coaches

ASCA-certified and qualified in Performance Nutrition through Clean Health Institute, Shobana combines evidence-based strength work with clear structure so clients know exactly what they are training for and why it matters.

Her experience spans general population clients, recreational athletes, and competitive performers, including people managing injuries or medical conditions. The goal is always the same: build strength, resilience, and better performance in a way that is safe enough to last.
