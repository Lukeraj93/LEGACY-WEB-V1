Things You Didn't Know About Jenita

Likes

Resilient people, honest effort, and the moment discipline starts to feel like identity instead of negotiation.

Dislikes

Waiting for perfect timing, half-committed effort, and treating fitness like something separate from the rest of life.

Pet Peeve

Calling consistency boring. Jenita knows the basics are usually where the real transformation starts.

Hobbies

Strength training, physique prep, studying nutrition, and building systems that help people elevate their standards.

Most People Wouldn't Guess

She spent 15 years in employment law, became a mother at 40, and still chose that season to go deeper into coaching rather than play smaller.

Quirk

Jenita can sound encouraging and uncompromising in the same sentence, which is exactly why clients keep moving.

How Jenita coaches

Backed by ACE Group Fitness and Personal Training certifications, Precision Nutrition Level 1, and more than 15 years of keeping high standards in a demanding legal career, Jenita coaches with both empathy and edge.

She is a strong fit for resilient, hard-working clients who want fitness woven into identity, not squeezed in temporarily, because her coaching keeps mindset, execution, and progression tied together.
