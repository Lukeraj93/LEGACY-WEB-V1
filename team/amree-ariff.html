Things You Didn't Know About Amree

Likes

Efficient training, strong fundamentals, and clients who value accountability enough to keep showing up.

Dislikes

Overcomplicating basics, skipping the patient work, and programmes that drain your life instead of supporting it.

Pet Peeve

Treating consistency like a mood. Amree would rather build a system you can repeat than chase one perfect week.

Hobbies

Olympic Weightlifting, strength training, nutrition, and refining better ways to coach complex movement simply.

Most People Wouldn't Guess

He spent over a decade in tech sales before coaching, which is part of why his sessions feel clear, efficient, and grounded in real-world schedules.

Quirk

He has a way of making hard work sound manageable, then you realise halfway through the session you are doing more than you expected.

How Amree coaches

What started as a hobby became competitive Olympic Weightlifting, and that journey shaped how Amree coaches today: master the fundamentals, build real strength, and move with purpose.

His style suits busy professionals especially well because he understands high-pressure work and the need for training that builds resilience and longevity without taking over the rest of life.
