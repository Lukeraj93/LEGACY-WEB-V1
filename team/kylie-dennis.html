Coach Profile

Kylie Dennis

Kylie is a yoga teacher and mobility-focused movement coach who specialises in older adults, beginners, and people navigating injuries, chronic pain, or pregnancy. Her sessions are supportive, injury-aware, and inclusive, with calm structure that helps clients feel safe enough to progress.

Things You Didn't Know About Kylie

Likes

Clear movement, thoughtful modifications, and seeing someone leave feeling more capable than when they arrived.

Dislikes

Needless chaos, rushed progressions, and any environment that makes beginners feel like they have to prove themselves first.

Pet Peeve

When people think needing an option or modification means they are behind. Kylie sees that as smart coaching, not a setback.

Hobbies

Mobility work, accessible movement education, and helping people find strength without fighting their own bodies.

Most People Wouldn't Guess

She is a former studio owner and teacher trainer, so behind the calm presence is years of building classes, coaching systems, and mentoring other teachers.

Quirk

Kylie can usually spot the one small adjustment that makes a movement feel safer almost immediately.

How Kylie coaches

With more than a decade of teaching experience and advanced training in injury management, accessible yoga, and pre/postnatal care, Kylie builds sessions around joint health, posture, mobility, and functional strength.

She is especially effective with older adults, beginners, and anyone returning from injury or working around chronic pain or pregnancy, because her coaching creates progress without unnecessary intimidation.
