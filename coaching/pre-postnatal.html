Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions before starting.

Do I need previous training experience?

No. The program works well for both active clients and those returning after a long break.

Will sessions change week to week?

Yes. We adjust session demands around how you are feeling, how recovery is going, and what your body is tolerating well.

Is this medical treatment?

No. This is private coaching. When needed, we recommend clients coordinate with the relevant healthcare guidance before training.

Can you help me return to regular strength training later?

Yes. One of the main goals of this service is to help you rebuild toward confident, sustainable training when the timing is right.

See More..
Can I start during pregnancy even if I have not trained much before?

Yes. We scale the session demands to your actual baseline and build confidence from there.

Will every session feel low intensity?

No. The point is not to make training soft. It is to make it appropriate, well-timed, and safe for the stage you are in.

Do you coordinate around sleep disruption and energy dips postpartum?

Yes. That is one of the main reasons private coaching works well here. We dose the work around recovery reality, not ideal conditions.

Can my partner or family member help with the consultation?

Yes. If having a partner involved makes the planning process easier, we can accommodate that in the consult.

What if I feel nervous about restarting exercise?

That is common. The first phase is designed to remove uncertainty and rebuild confidence before we push harder.

How do I know which coach is right for me?

We use the consultation to match you by pace, communication style, current stage, and the type of support you respond best to.
