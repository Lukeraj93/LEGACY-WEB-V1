Frequently Asked Questions

What clients usually want to know before starting.

Do I need a strong training background before I start?

No. We coach both experienced clients and people returning after a long gap. The program starts from your actual baseline.

How often should I train to see progress?

That depends on your goal, schedule, and recovery. We usually recommend the most sustainable starting frequency during the consultation.

Can you help if I travel often or have an unpredictable work calendar?

Yes. Many private clients need a plan that stays structured even when the week is not. That is one of the main reasons they choose this service.

Is the consultation mainly a sales call?

No. The point is to understand your goal, clarify fit, and recommend the right first move, even if that means a smaller starting package.

See More..
Can I start with one session a week and scale later?

Yes. Many clients start with the lowest-friction option first, then increase frequency once the routine is stable.

Do you help with body composition as well as strength?

Yes. We regularly coach clients who want fat loss, muscle gain, better energy, and stronger performance at the same time.

Can I request a specific coach from the start?

Yes. If you already know who you want to work with, we can factor that into the consult and schedule match.

What if my work schedule changes every week?

That is common. We plan around realistic availability and adjust early rather than letting inconsistency pile up.

Do you track progress beyond the session itself?

Yes. We look at training quality, progression markers, recovery, and practical wins outside the gym as well.

How quickly will I know if the service is a good fit?

Usually within the consultation. We can tell quite quickly whether your goals, schedule, and preferred pace fit the service.
