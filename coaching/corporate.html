How Coaching Starts

How We Scope The Right Format For Your Team

The process starts with the brief, then moves into format design so the workshop feels relevant before it ever gets delivered.

  1. 01 Step 1

    Discovery Call

    We review team size, audience profile, current pain points, and what the business actually wants the session to solve.

    Business context first
  2. 02 Step 2

    Team Brief Review

    We map schedule, location, energy level, and practical constraints so the session fits the way your team actually works.

    Audience + logistics
  3. 03 Step 3

    Format Design

    We recommend the right structure, duration, and workshop focus so the content lands clearly instead of feeling generic.

    Right-fit recommendation
  4. 04 Step 4

    Session Delivery

    We run the workshop on-site, at a partner venue, or in a private setting depending on the audience and format chosen.

    Live delivery
  5. 05 Step 5

    Follow-Through Plan

    If the team needs more than one touchpoint, we map the next session, reinforcement rhythm, and any recurring support required.

    Series or one-off next steps

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions companies usually ask first.

Can this be delivered at our office?

Yes. We can deliver on-site or help scope a private venue depending on the session type and team size.

Do you only run fitness sessions?

No. The format can combine movement education, recovery habits, posture guidance, and performance-focused wellness themes.

Is this suitable for leadership teams?

Yes. We can scope a more executive-focused format for smaller leadership groups who want a more tailored experience.

Can we start with one workshop and expand later?

Yes. Many companies begin with a single workshop before deciding whether to roll out a wider series.

Can you tailor the session to desk-based teams?

Yes. That is one of the most common briefs we receive, and the content can be shaped around posture, stiffness, energy, and recovery.

Do you provide executive-only formats?

Yes. We can scope smaller, more private sessions for leadership groups who want a more premium format.

Once we have the brief, we can usually recommend a direction quickly and then formalize the proposal after that.
