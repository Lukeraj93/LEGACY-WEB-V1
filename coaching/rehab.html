Rebuild Strength After Injury.

1:1 coaching for clients who want to return to normal with a structured rehab path built around strength training.

What This Service Delivers

Rehab coaching that helps you move confidently again, not just avoid pain.

This program is for clients who have sustained injuries and want to rebuild normal movement, restore confidence, and return to training safely. We use progressive strength training to strengthen the body, improve tolerance to load, and give you a clear path back to daily life and regular exercise.

Progressive strength rehab 1:1 return-to-function coaching Coach-led load management

Best fit clients

  • Clients recovering from injuries who want a structured return to normal movement
  • Adults who feel unsure about training again after pain or setbacks
  • People who need guided strength work instead of random rehab exercises
  • Clients who want coach oversight, progression, and accountability during recovery

The rehab pathway is led by a head coach with physiotherapy and strength and conditioning expertise, so the work stays practical, progressive, and grounded in return-to-function outcomes.

How Coaching Starts

What Happens After You Book a Rehab Consult

The process is structured to understand the injury, set a realistic baseline, and rebuild from there.

  1. 01 Step 1

    Consult Call

    We cover injury background, current limitations, pain behaviour, and the goals you need the body to handle again.

    Clear starting picture
  2. 02 Step 2

    Movement Assessment

    We assess movement range, strength tolerance, and control so we know what needs rebuilding first.

    Function audit
  3. 03 Step 3

    Coach Match

    We assign the right coach and rehab direction so the work matches your injury history, pace, and return target.

    Rehab-specific support
  4. 04 Step 4

    Strength Rebuild

    Coached sessions rebuild strength through controlled loading, cleaner movement, and week-to-week progressions you can trust.

    Rebuild capacity
  5. 05 Step 5

    Return to Normal

    We guide the transition back into daily function, regular training, or sport prep without rushing the final phase.

    Confident next step

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions before starting rehab coaching.

Is this only for serious injuries?

No. It is for clients recovering from both major and minor injuries who need a structured return to normal movement and training.

Do you use strength training even if I feel deconditioned?

Yes. Strength training is scaled to your actual baseline so the body gets stronger without jumping ahead of what you can tolerate.

Can this help me return to regular gym training?

Yes. One of the main goals is to bridge you back into normal training with more confidence, better movement quality, and less guesswork.

Will every session feel like rehab drills only?

No. The coaching uses rehab principles, but the work still aims to make you stronger, more capable, and more normal in how you move.

See More..
Can I join if I am still nervous about pain coming back?

Yes. That is common. The process is built to restore confidence gradually with controlled progressions and coach feedback.

Is this medical treatment?

No. This is coaching. If you are under medical care, we work within that context and build the training plan around safe progression.

How do you decide which coach is best for me?

We look at the injury context, your confidence level, your training background, and the coaching style that will give you the best adherence.

What if I am not ready for intense training yet?

That is fine. We start from the current baseline and build intensity only when control and tolerance are there.

Can rehab coaching also improve strength long term?

Yes. The goal is not only to recover. It is to come back stronger, more resilient, and better prepared for future training.

How quickly can I start after the consult?

Usually within a week, depending on coach availability and how quickly we complete the assessment and matching process.
