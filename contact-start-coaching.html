Frequently Asked Questions

What happens after you contact us

How quickly will someone get back to me?

Most enquiries receive a reply within 24 hours, and WhatsApp is usually the fastest route.

Do I need to know which package or coach I want first?

No. Tell us your goal, schedule, and training background, and we will help you narrow the right fit.

What if I am enquiring for pre and postnatal or older adult coaching?

Include your current stage, health considerations, and any guidance you have already received so we can plan the safest first step.

Can I enquire for a company or leadership team?

Yes. Share your team size, preferred format, and whether you need a single workshop, series, or recurring support.
