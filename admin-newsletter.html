Writing Standard

How The Weekly Issue Should Read

Keep the tone community-first, energetic, and concise. Each issue should sound like it came from a real coach-led business, not a generic promo engine.

  • Choose one tone per issue: motivational or professional.
  • Open with the member’s registered first name so the email feels written to a person, not a list.
  • Lead with a strong subject line that feels fitness-focused and worth opening.
  • Open with a warm greeting, then move straight into the week’s important updates.
  • Use clear bullets for schedule changes, new coaches, offers, or announcements.
  • Include one short member spotlight or practical coaching tip.
  • Back technical training or nutrition claims with 1 to 2 credible references.
  • Close with a simple call to action that gets members back into the gym.
